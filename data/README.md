# Data

## Included

`country_data.csv`: AI adoption and labour productivity for 23 European economies, compiled for this analysis.
- **AI adoption:** Eurostat, table `isoc_eb_ai` (indicator `E_AI_TANY`, % of enterprises with 10+ employees using at least one AI technology), 2024.
- **Productivity:** OECD, GDP per hour worked, 2023, USD PPP.

The US and Northern Ireland are not in this file: the US isn't covered by Eurostat, and Northern Ireland isn't reported separately from the UK. They are handled separately, with caveats, in the charts.

## Not included — download separately

The Oxford Insights **Government AI Readiness Index** spreadsheets are not redistributed here. Download them from [Oxford Insights](https://oxfordinsights.com/ai-readiness/ai-readiness-index/) and save them in this folder with these names:

- `2025-Government-AI-Readiness-Index-data-1.xlsx`
- `2024-GAIRI-data.xlsx`
- `2023-Government-AI-Readiness-Index-Public-Indicator-Data.xlsx`

## Other figures

Charts 1–3 use headline figures typed directly into the scripts from published reports: the Stanford AI Index 2025, OECD, the UK Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT), the US Census Bureau's Business Trends and Outlook Survey, and AICC/Invest NI. Each script's header notes its sources.
